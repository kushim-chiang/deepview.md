# DeepView MD (WFMarkdownReader) Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 6, 2026

Thank you for using DeepView MD (the “App” or “we”). We value your privacy and data security. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data, and the rights you can exercise. If you do not agree with this policy, please stop using the App.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to your use of the App on iOS/iPadOS, including file browsing, Markdown editing, AI assistant, export, and sharing features.

## 2. Data We Collect

The App is designed with a “local-first” approach.

- **Essential device data (local)**: File paths, filenames, and editing content (stored only on your device).
- **Preferences (local)**: Font size, templates, custom styles, and other preferences stored in local settings or files.
- **AI request content (optional, cloud)**: When you use the AI assistant, the text, snippets, or prompts you choose to send will be processed by the AI model service provider.
- **Diagnostics (local or optional)**: If you choose to share error reports or logs, they may include crash stacks, OS version, and device model; nothing is uploaded by default.

## 3. How We Use Data

- Provide and maintain services: Read/write Markdown files you select, render previews, export, and share.
- AI generation: When you actively submit content, we send it to the chosen AI model service provider to obtain a response.
- Security and debugging: With your consent, diagnostics help us troubleshoot issues.

## 4. Storage and Retention

- Local files and preferences stay on your device unless you back them up or sync them (e.g., iCloud Drive).
- AI request content may be temporarily stored by the third-party AI service provider to complete inference; retention follows the provider’s policy.
- We do not retain your content long-term on our own servers.

## 5. Permissions

- **File access**: To open, edit, import, and export the files or folders you choose.
- **Network access**: Used only when the AI assistant or sharing/export requires uploads.
- **Clipboard access**: Accessed only when you explicitly choose “Include Clipboard Content.”

## 6. Third-Party Services

- **AI model service provider**: When you use the AI assistant, content is sent to the model service (e.g., cloud inference API). The provider may process, temporarily store, or use it for security detection per their privacy policy. We do not share this content with other third parties.
- **OS cloud sync**: If you use iCloud Drive or OS-level sync, file and preference synchronization is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

## 7. Data Security

- The App does not send your file content to unauthorized third parties without encryption.
- AI transmissions use HTTPS. Please avoid submitting confidential or personal data in prompts.

## 8. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. If you are under 13, please use the App only with guardian consent and avoid submitting personal data.

## 9. Your Rights

- **Access and correction**: Open, edit, or delete files and templates on your device.
- **Disable AI transmissions**: Do not use the AI assistant to avoid sending content to third parties.
- **Delete local data**: Remove the App or delete related files to clear local data.

## 10. Policy Updates

We may update this policy due to feature changes or legal requirements. Updates will be reflected in this document with the effective date. For material changes, we will notify you in-app or via another appropriate method.

## 11. Contact Us

If you have privacy questions or requests, please contact us via the channel listed on the App Store page.
